Projects / LiveView

LiveView

Watch live golf from courses near you.

A phone app for streams coming off a golf course. The course points a camera at a hole, a match, or a member’s round. You open a directory, see what is on right now, and watch. When the round is over, the short highlights stay up.

Open LiveView Take the tour

Open LiveView is the running app. Take the tour is a static walkthrough: invented courses and license-free clips. The tour is not a live broadcast, and it does not need an account.

Who it’s for

Golfers who can’t be there

A club event, a friend’s round, a nine-hole match on a Tuesday. Find the course and watch it from somewhere else.

Courses

Register the course, set up a stream, and put an event or a member’s round on the live-now lane.

See it

Short recordings of the demo. The directory, a course stream, saved highlights, and a watch party.

What’s in it

  • Live now

    A lane of courses that are streaming, in front of the rest of the directory.

  • Directory and map

    Search, then filter by city and state. Each course has a shareable page.

  • Stream setup

    A course registers and gets an RTMP ingest and an HLS playback URL. New streams wait for approval.

  • Replays

    Short highlight clips stay on the course page after the round.

  • Feed and watch parties

    An activity feed, and a room where a few people watch the same stream.

  • On a phone

    Installable, with caching for pages it has already loaded, and a layout that fits a small screen.
