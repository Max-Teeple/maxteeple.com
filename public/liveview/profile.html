Live stream setup

Fastest setup path

  1. Use a phone with a stable tripod for a starter setup, or a camera with HDMI out plus a capture card for a cleaner production feed.
  2. Stream to YouTube Live, Twitch, or Vimeo — or publish a public HLS (.m3u8) URL. Cloudflare Stream and RTMP ingest are not on the free plan.
  3. Paste that link into Stream link and save. Optionally generate a webhook key if your encoder will POST live/offline updates.
  4. Request LiveView approval. After approval, use the stream toggle to mark the broadcast live or offline for fans.

Recommended hardware: phone or mirrorless camera, tripod, external battery, microphone if commentary matters, reliable Wi-Fi or wired internet, and shade/weather protection for outdoor setups.

Regenerating the webhook key breaks any encoder already posting status updates with the old key. It does not change your YouTube, Twitch, Vimeo, or HLS link.
